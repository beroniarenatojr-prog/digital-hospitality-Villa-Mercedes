# Villa Mercedes guest knowledge

Villa Mercedes Nature Farm & Resort is in Barangay Namnama, City of Ilagan, Isabela, Philippines. Main contact: 0917 326 8271. Email: villamercedesresort@yahoo.com. Facebook: Villa Mercedes Nature Farm & Resort.

## Public pools and cottages

The resort has two public pools: an Adult Pool and a Kiddie Pool. A separate Private Pool is available only through selected packages; ask staff which package includes it.

Day swimming is 9:00 AM–5:00 PM. Entrance is ₱100 per adult and ₱70 for a child below 4 feet. Night swimming is 5:00 PM–10:00 PM and costs ₱120 per swimmer. Children are not allowed to swim during night swimming. Pool entrance is paid at the resort entrance.

There are 12 numbered cottages. Guests can choose an available cottage online for a date and swimming session. Cottage rental is ₱500 per cottage per session. Pool entrance is separate and is not included in the cottage reservation total. Cottages have no stated guest-capacity limit, but the booking records the expected party size for staff preparation. Availability is not guaranteed until the reservation is submitted and approved.

Key pool rules: shower first; supervise children; wear proper swimwear; no food or drinks in the pool area; no alcohol or glass around the pool; no pets; no running, rough play, diving, pushing, bombing, shouting, or throwing objects; use the restroom; and follow posted staff safety directions. Drinks, appliances, and electronics may have fees where permitted.

## Booking and safe answers

Browsing and availability checks are public. To reserve an available room, hall, package, or cottage, sign in or create an account. The selected service, dates, party size, and cottage session are kept through login and signup. Authentication returns the guest directly to that service's reservation form. Email-only booking confirmation links are no longer used. Public swimming entrance remains payable at the entrance.

Rooms, cottages, function halls, and selected packages can be requested through the website. A pending request is not a confirmed booking. Staff verify availability, approval, and payment. Do not promise a date, refund, exception, discount, weather outcome, lifeguard coverage, accessibility feature, or unlisted amenity.
