# Reports and insights: interpretation rules

Updated November 18, 2026.

The four report views keep their existing data definitions. Findings are calculated from the same results as their totals and charts, and included in CSV and printable exports.

- Bookings: compare service-reservation count; state cancellation count and percentage, confirmed/completed count and value, and the leading service category. Fewer than ten records triggers a descriptive small-sample note. This is a presentation safeguard, not a statistical significance test.
- Completed services: compare delivered service value and identify the service type contributing the largest share. Completion requires recorded completion or checkout evidence; the period uses scheduled checkout, consistently with the existing report.
- Payments: compare verified receipts, explain receipts less returned refunds, and identify the leading payment method. Negative net receipts are explained without treating them as operating losses.
- Balances: identify the amount and share unpaid after checkout, flag overpayments, and show unpaid amounts by stay status. There is no invented historical comparison.

The comparison is the immediately preceding equal number of inclusive calendar days, with the same source, service filter and date basis. A November 1–18 selection compares against October 14–31, not the entire prior month. Current booking/payment statuses apply to both periods; approval/status history is not reconstructed. Future-ended periods do not receive elapsed-period performance comparisons.

Zero baseline never produces infinite or 100% growth. A zero recorded total does not prove the earlier data was complete. Tied leading categories are identified as tied. Findings do not attribute causes, forecast demand, or prescribe price changes.

Automatic chart interval: up to 31 days uses daily buckets; up to 120 uses weekly; up to 1,096 uses monthly; longer selections use yearly. Staff may explicitly choose another interval. Existing long-range daily/weekly safeguards remain. Quick ranges select Automatic. Missing dates default to month-to-date; the visible effective chart interval appears above the chart.

The reports deliberately separate booking value, completed service value and verified cash receipts. The original research used Cloudbeds' payment ledger and account balances documentation and the CoStar/STR definitions of occupancy, ADR and RevPAR. The implemented findings make no hotel benchmark claims; historical available-room inventory and daily room-revenue allocation are not sufficiently recorded for those benchmarks.
